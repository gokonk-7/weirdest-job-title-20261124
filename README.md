**The entry: "Professional Mourner for Strangers' Goldfish"** (44 characters).

Professional mourners are a real occupation — but this one is hired to grieve goldfish belonging to complete strangers: fish they never met, for people they never met. A person in black, weeping over a tiny fishbowl, on the clock. The twist lands on the first read.
